# ADR-011: Send alerts by email through SNS

Status: proposed
Date: 2026-10-29

Context: Budget, security and contact alerts need to reach me reliably at
low cost.

Options:
- SNS email to a dedicated address: simple and very low cost; email can be
  missed or ignored.
- Chat integration (such as Slack): faster to notice; extra setup and an
  external service to secure.
- SMS through SNS: immediate; charges apply and extra registration is
  required.

Decision: Use SNS topics with email subscriptions to a dedicated alert
address, separate from the contact-form topic.

Consequences:
- Cost: email notifications cost nothing at this volume (Confirmed with AWS
  Pricing Calculator).
- Risk: alert fatigue and missed messages; mitigated by alerting only on
  medium severity or higher and by checking the inbox on a fixed routine.
- Separation: the alert address is not used for any account root user.
