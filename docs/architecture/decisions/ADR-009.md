# ADR-009: Publish in English first, Spanish later

Status: proposed
Date: 2026-10-29

Context: The target audience includes recruiters and security teams who
read English, and Spanish pages widen the audience. Producing both from
the start would slow the first release.

Options:
- English only: fastest, smaller audience.
- Spanish only: wrong fit for the target roles.
- Both from the start: widest audience; doubles the writing before launch.
- English first, Spanish in a future phase: ships sooner and still reaches
  both.

Decision: Launch in English and add Spanish pages in next phase. Write-ups
and lab reports stay in English, since English is the working language of
the SOC roles I am targeting.

Consequences:
- Structure: plan URLs and Hugo language folders in the early stages so
  Spanish pages can be added without breaking links.
- Effort: Spanish pages and a language switcher are added later, and the
  accessibility check is repeated.
