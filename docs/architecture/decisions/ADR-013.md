# ADR-013: Control spending with Budgets, tags and a monthly cost log

Status: accepted
Date: 2026-11-06

Context: The project has a hard limit of $5 a month, and the Paid plan
(see ADR-003) means charges are real from day one. Forgotten lab resources
are the main risk, and AWS shows costs with a delay of about a day.

Options:
- AWS Budgets alerts only: automatic, but they say that spending is high,
  not why, and keep no history.
- Check the Billing console occasionally: no setup; easy to forget, leaves
  no record and cannot show a trend.
- Budgets and Cost Anomaly Detection, resource tags, and a written monthly
  cost log: early warnings plus a record of causes and actions; more
  routine work each month.
- A third-party cost tool: richer reports; another account to secure and
  often a cost of its own.

Decision: Use AWS Budgets (alerts at $1, $3 and $5, plus a forecast
alert), Cost Anomaly Detection, the tags `project` and `lifecycle`, and a
monthly entry in `docs/cost-log.md` that reconciles every charge with a
budget line.

Consequences:
- Cost: all of these are free to use, and the log takes a few minutes each
  month.
- Evidence: the log shows each month at or under $5 and what I did when an
  alert fired, which supports the plan's cost goal.
- Limits: alerts depend on billing data that lags, so the lab reaper and
  the destroy-and-verify step cover forgotten resources between alerts.
- Upkeep: the tags must be activated for cost allocation in the Billing
  console and every resource must carry them; the default tags in
  Terraform handle this.
