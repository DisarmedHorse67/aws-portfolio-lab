# ADR-006: Register the domain through Route 53

Status: accepted
Date: 2026-10-29

Context: The site needs a memorable HTTPS address, and the ACM certificate
is validated through DNS records.

Options:
- Default CloudFront address: free, but not memorable or professional, and
  no practice with DNS and certificates.
- Domain registered in Route 53: registration and DNS in one place, alias
  records to CloudFront are free of query charges; about $12 a year for a
  .com, plus $0.50 a month for the hosted zone.
- Domain from another registrar: sometimes cheaper; adds the step of
  pointing name servers to Route 53 or managing DNS elsewhere.

Decision: Register the domain in Route 53 so registration, DNS and
certificate validation are managed in one console and in Terraform.
Subdomains will be used to host this and future secondary projects.

Consequences:
- Cost: roughly $1 a month amortized, already in the budget; AWS credits
  cannot pay for domain registration, so it is paid by card.
- Upkeep: turn on auto-renew and the transfer lock, and check whether the
  TLD includes privacy protection.
- Terraform: the hosted zone created by the registration is imported, not
  recreated, to avoid paying for a second zone.
- Structure: The apex domain will remain clear for a primary project
  while this project will be hosted on a subdomain (e.g., 'lab-dcc.cybergulio.com') for isolation from other secondary projects without incurring additional domain registration fees.
